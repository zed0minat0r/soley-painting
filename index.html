V · Questions

Before you book.

How does the estimate work?

Call or text Sean at (484) 948-5573, or send the form below. He comes out, measures every surface, and follows up with a written quote, line by line. It's free.

Will you protect my floors and furniture?

Yes. Drop cloths on every floor in the work area, covers on anything that can't be moved, and tape on trim and outlets. We tidy up at the end of every day.

What paint do you use?

Professional-grade Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams, matched to the surface: harder enamels for high-traffic areas, flat for ceilings, and cabinet-grade finishes for cabinets.

What if something goes wrong?

We're fully insured on every job. If a finish peels or shows a defect from our work, we come back and make it right at no charge.
